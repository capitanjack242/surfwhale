What it does

Everything you need to scout a whale

📊

Portfolio Analytics

Net worth, open positions, spot balances, funding fees, and an all-time equity curve for any Hyperliquid wallet — computed from real fill history, not a snapshot.

📈

Trade Performance

Win rate, profit factor, drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino, and a full day-by-day P&L breakdown — the kind of stats a serious trader actually checks.

🔭

Whale Glass

An always-on scanner that finds real active traders worth watching — filtered by actual trading activity, not just a big balance sitting in staking.

🧪

Sim Account

Paper-trade a whale's exact moves scaled to your own capital — live going forward, or backtested against their entire trade history.

🐋

Copy Trading

The next step: turn a monitored whale into an actual scaled-down, automated position follower. Coming soon.
