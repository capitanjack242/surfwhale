Whales long is the share of dollars held long among the whales we track that have a position in that market, with the number of those whales after it. Each whale's positions are re-read about twice a day, so this is a stance, not a tick-by-tick feed. Markets with fewer than three whales show a dash.

OI 24h and OI 7d compare open interest in dollars with our own hourly samples. Hyperliquid only publishes the current figure, so a market shows a dash until we have a sample that old.

Listed is the market's first funding payment, which lands within an hour of the listing. Type tags on builder-deployed markets are Surf Whale's own classification.
